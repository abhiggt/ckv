# Changelog

Every release of Chakrview and Chakrvroom, newest first. The version number also appears in each game under Settings → What's new.

Version numbers follow `major.minor.patch`:
- **Patch (1.0.1):** a fix.
- **Minor (1.1.0):** something new to play.
- **Major (2.0.0):** a big change in how the games feel.

---

## 1.0.0 · 12 November 2026 · First release

### Chakrview

- Fifty-six mazes across seven worlds, each on its own carved board of Indian craft.
- Two ways to play: **Journey**, through every world in order, and **Relax**, endless gentle mazes with no clock and no holes.
- Your best time is kept on every level. Arrive within the flow time and a lotus petal is yours.
- Worlds that slowly come alive: a ring that flows, holes that glow, a passing breeze, posts that sing, doors that rise and fall.
- Something inside the glass marble, for you to discover.
- Low, warm music with a soft pulse beneath it, made to slow you down. Headphones help.
- Tilt or drag, switchable any time from the play bar, with music on or off right beside it.
- Sky and Path views.
- A pause menu with restart, settings and quick sound toggles.
- A short guide on first play.
- Three marbles (Kancha, Peetal, Fauladi) that grow steadier the more you roll them.
- Installs to the home screen and plays offline.

### Chakrvroom

- Six tracks, one for each enemy within, from **Krodh** (anger) to **Ahankar** (ego), each harder than the last.
- Quick races against five rivals (Jhatka, Nazar, Chaska, Shor, Dhuan), each with its own way of racing, mistakes included.
- The race ends when every rival is in. If you're still out there, you can choose to complete your journey.
- Rooms for up to four friends, with your own four-letter code or a random one. A code already in use is turned away.
- Laps, then a maze: the gate opens on the final lap and the centre is the finish.
- Lap times, your fastest lap, overtake calls, a final-lap bell and a low, driving beat.
- Tilt or drag, and music on or off, right from the race bar.
